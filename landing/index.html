Run your dog-walking business from one place.

Sit.Stay.Play is business management software for professional dog walkers and pet-care providers. Keep client and pet records, send service agreements for signature, schedule visits, invoice, and get paid by card — without stitching together a notebook, a calendar, and a payment app.

What it does

Everything below is built and in use. This is a working product, not a waitlist.

Clients and pets

Every client, their pets, breeds, and the details you need on a walk — searchable, in one record instead of five places.

Service agreements

Generate a pet services agreement, have the client sign it, and keep the signed copy locked and on file.

Scheduling

Book one-off visits or repeating weekly work. Double-bookings are refused rather than discovered later.

Invoicing and card payments

Completed walks turn into invoices. Clients pay by card through Stripe, and the money goes to your own account.

Messaging

Talk to clients in the app, with a record of what was agreed. Messages written offline send when you reconnect.

A portal for pet owners

Clients get their own view to review and sign agreements, pay invoices, see upcoming visits, and message you.

Plans

A free, ad-supported plan and a paid plan without ads. Full pricing is still being finalised — get in touch and we will tell you exactly where it lands before you commit to anything.

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Sit.Stay.Play never sees or stores your clients' card numbers, and your bank details are entered on Stripe's own secure pages rather than ours.
